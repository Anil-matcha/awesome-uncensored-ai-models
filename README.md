# Awesome Uncensored AI Models

> An index of source-tracked catalogs for low-filter, abliterated, and community-reported AI models across language, image, and video generation.

“Uncensored” is not a standardized technical guarantee. Behavior depends on the exact weights, fine-tune or adapter, inference pipeline, interface, hosted provider, and version. The companion catalogs record that evidence and distinguish local weights from hosted access.

## Inclusion principles

- Record the exact model/checkpoint or hosted route and the date verified.
- Distinguish open weights, community fine-tunes, and hosted endpoints.
- Treat “uncensored” as a qualified behavior report, not a promise or a license.
- Link to primary model cards and provider documentation; do not infer derivative provenance from the base model.
- Track licensing, availability, filtering layers, and reproducibility where known.
- Keep detailed model tables in the modality catalog that owns them; this repository is the directory, not a duplicate mirror.

## Contributing
